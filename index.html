Which stone is right for you

All three make a great countertop. The difference is how much care you want to give it.

Comparison of quartz, granite and quartzite countertops
Quartz Granite Quartzite
What it is Engineered from crushed quartz and resin Natural stone, quarried in one piece Natural stone, sandstone hardened under heat and pressure
Upkeep No sealing. Wipe with soap and water Seal about once a year Seal about once a year
Heat Use a trivet. Resin can scorch Handles hot pans well Handles hot pans well
Look Consistent color, Calacatta and marble looks Speckled and flowing patterns, every slab unique Marble-like veining with far more hardness
Best for Busy family kitchens, baths, rentals Cooks who want durability and natural pattern Statement islands and waterfall ends

Eased

From slab to sink in four steps

You deal with the same crew from the first measure to the final wipe-down.

  1. Pick your stone

    Bring us a photo, a sample or a slab you love. We help you choose a color and edge that suits the room and your budget.

  2. We measure on site

    Once your cabinets are set, we template every wall, corner and appliance so the stone fits the room you actually have.

  3. We cut and polish

    Your slab is cut, seamed and edged, with sink and cooktop openings made in the shop, not in your kitchen.

  4. We install

    We set the tops, mount the sink and seal the seams. Most kitchens are finished in a single day.
